# OpenTable reference observations — Alesyan Reservations

The user shared screenshots of their restaurant's existing OpenTable experience on 12 October 2026, including front-of-house floor plan, staff menus, booking widget configurator, restaurant profile and pricing. Do NOT reuse customer personal information in any dataset, tests or demo.

## Relevant workflows
- Floor map and reservation/guest list appear together. The live floor view marks occupied tables and includes seated/finished sections with elapsed time.
- The diary needs walk-in seating, guest check-in, seated and finished states, and a chronological service timeline.
- Navigation includes list, floor map, timeline, calendar, shift overview, guestbook, staff sections and management.
- Web management navigation includes availability planning, private dining, reporting, restaurant profile/settings, policies, integration, user accounts, communications and marketing.
- A configurable website widget has theme/customisation, language, overlay vs separate-page behaviour and attribution/tracking.
- Restaurant profile supports descriptive information, cuisine, images, menus, phone, restaurant website, and opening details.
- Reservation waitlist should convert to an actual booking atomically and retain a history trail.
- Alesyan must keep its own original UX and privacy design. Do not copy icons, artwork, proprietary text or styling.
